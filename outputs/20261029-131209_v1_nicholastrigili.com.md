# Interact demo prep: Nicholas Trigili

*Based only on the website text provided. I haven't verified anything off-site, so confirm details on the call. The contact info looks like test data (example.com).*

## Company snapshot

- **What they sell:** High-ticket, application-based online coaching for "high-performing men" who want a "top 1% physique". It covers training, nutrition, recovery and lifestyle, with 3, 6 and 12 month programs.
- **Founder brand:** Nicholas Trigili is a former IFBB Pro with 20+ years of experience, positioned as a "performance & biohacking specialist". The brand leans on a premium, data-driven, longevity-minded identity.
- **Audience:** Men who want to look and perform at a high level into their 40s and 50s. Likely executives, founders and professionals who are time-poor and status- and results-oriented.
- **Process:** Assessment → protocol engineering → 30/60/90-day execution → longevity systems.
- **Upsells:** Bloodwork is included in the 6 and 12 month tiers. Optional TRT, peptides and medical supervision are billed separately by the partner TruGenetics.
- **Current funnel:** Nearly every CTA is "Apply Now" or "Apply to work with us", plus a walkthrough video and a "resources" lead magnet. Social proof is a wall of transformations.

## The likely problem

The note ("get more qualified applicants") suggests a typical high-ticket coaching issue:
- A single "Apply" CTA treats every visitor the same, so the application gets a mix of tire-kickers, budget-limited leads, and people who aren't a fit.
- The setters or closers likely spend time on unqualified calls.
- Visitors who aren't ready to apply have no middle step, and the resources offer is generic.

## Quiz ideas (in priority order)

### 1. "Top 1% Performance Score" (recommended primary quiz)
A diagnostic that mirrors Step 1 of their process, the Comprehensive Assessment.

- **Score on 5 pillars**, matching their system graphic: Training, Nutrition, Recovery/Sleep, Stress/Lifestyle, and Data/Tracking.
- **Sample questions:**
  - Primary goal (lose fat, build muscle, both, maintain as I age)
  - Age range
  - Training history and current frequency
  - Average sleep and stress level
  - Do you track nutrition, sleep or bloodwork?
  - What have you tried before?
- **Qualifying questions at the end:**
  - Timeline ("I want to start within 30 days")
  - Level of investment they're comfortable with in coaching
  - Willingness to do weekly check-ins and share data
- **Results:** A personalized score, the weakest pillar, and a short explanation.
- **Routing:**
  - High fit → "Apply now" with the application or calendar embedded on the results page
  - Medium fit → a walkthrough video plus nurture emails
  - Low fit → a free resource and a lower-touch path
- **Why it works:** Applicants arrive pre-educated, and the sales team sees their answers before the call.

### 2. "Are You Ready to Work With Us?" (readiness and fit quiz)
A shorter, more direct screening quiz to replace or sit in front of the application.
- It covers commitment, budget range, past coaching experience, and whether they'll follow a protocol.
- It suits cold traffic from ads, where qualification matters most.
- It can disqualify politely and send those people to resources instead.

### 3. "What's Holding Back Your Physique?" (archetype quiz)
A more shareable, top-of-funnel quiz for social and ad traffic.
- **Outcomes:** The Overtrainer, The Busy Executive, The Inconsistent Dieter, The Under-Recovered Performer, The Plateaued Veteran.
- Each outcome comes with tailored advice and a matching CTA.
- It gives them segmentation data for email and retargeting.

### 4. "Which Resource Should You Start With?" (resource matcher)
It turns the generic "High Performance Resources" section into a personalized recommendation, which should lift opt-in rates.

### 5. "Energy & Longevity Audit" (optional, handle with care)
It's aimed at men 35+ and covers energy, recovery and sleep, and it could lead into the bloodwork conversation. It's a strong fit with their positioning, but see the compliance notes below.

## Compliance and brand notes

- **Don't diagnose or recommend TRT, peptides or medical treatment in quiz results.** Frame results as lifestyle and performance insights, and say that bloodwork and medical decisions go through a licensed provider.
- **Health data:** Ask whether TruGenetics has requirements on collecting or passing along health information. Avoid asking for detailed medical history in the quiz.
- **Results claims:** Their site already uses a disclaimer ("Individual results vary"). Reuse similar language on results pages.
- **Tone:** Keep it premium and data-driven, not gimmicky. The design should feel like a diagnostic, not a BuzzFeed quiz.

## Where to place it
- Replace or supplement the many "Apply Now" buttons with "Take the Performance Assessment".
- Instagram or YouTube bio links and ad landing pages.
- After the walkthrough video.
- As the gate to the resources download.

## Demo talking points
- Show lead scoring and outcome routing, so qualified leads go straight to the application or calendar and others go to nurture.
- Show how answers pass into their CRM or email tool as tags and fields, so the closer sees the lead's goals and objections before the call.
- Show conditional logic: if budget or commitment is low, show different results.
- Show the quiz embedded on the page or as a popup, and mention A/B testing.
- Pitch the metrics: application volume vs. quality, show rate, close rate, and cost per qualified applicant.

## Discovery questions for the call
1. What does your current application look like, and what percentage of applicants are a fit?
2. Where does your traffic come from (organic, paid, YouTube, Instagram)?
3. How many calls does your team take per week, and what's the show and close rate?
4. What are the most common reasons you decline applicants?
5. What CRM, email, and scheduling tools do you use?
6. Do you want the quiz to gate the application, or sit alongside it?
7. Are there compliance constraints from the medical partner we should design around?
8. Is there a price range or program tier you'd like the quiz to push people toward?

## Suggested pitch
> "Based on your site, I'd start with a 'Top 1% Performance Score' quiz that mirrors your Step 1 assessment. Visitors get a personalized result, and you get structured data and a fit score on every lead, so your team talks to better-qualified applicants."
