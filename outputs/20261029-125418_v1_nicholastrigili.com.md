# Demo prep: Nicholas Trigili (Performance Coaching)

*Based only on the website text provided. I haven't verified anything beyond that.*

## Company snapshot
- **What they sell:** High-ticket, 1:1 online fitness and performance coaching for "high-performing men" who want a "top 1% physique" and longevity. It covers training, nutrition, recovery and lifestyle protocols, and weekly check-ins with 30/60/90-day milestones.
- **Founder brand:** Nicholas Trigili is a former IFBB Pro with 20+ years of experience. The brand is built on his authority, with "10,000 clients coached" and a large online audience.
- **Pricing structure:** Multiple program lengths. The 6- and 12-month programs include bloodwork through TruGenetics, a medical partner. TRT, peptides and supervised performance medicine are optional add-ons billed separately by the partner.
- **Conversion path:** Nearly every CTA is **"Apply"**, so it's an application funnel. There's also a "Watch the Walkthrough" video and a free resources section, which are likely the lead-capture points.
- **Likely buyer:** Men roughly 30-55, busy professionals or executives, already somewhat fitness-literate, with disposable income and interest in data, hormones and longevity.

## Their goal and the opportunity
They want more *qualified* applicants. The site is positioned as premium and exclusive, so the problem is probably unqualified applications (budget, commitment, fit) eating up sales-call time. A quiz can work as a **pre-qualification and segmentation layer** before the application or call.

## Quiz ideas

### 1. "Performance Profile Assessment" (primary recommendation)
**Role:** Top-of-funnel lead magnet that doubles as a qualifier. It mirrors Step 1 of their process ("Comprehensive Assessment"), so it feels like a natural preview of the service.
- **Sample questions:**
  - What's your primary goal? (fat loss, muscle, energy/performance, longevity)
  - Current training frequency?
  - Average sleep and stress level?
  - How have you tried to reach this goal before?
  - Have you worked with a coach before?
  - What's your biggest obstacle?
  - How soon do you want to start?
  - What level of investment are you comfortable with in a coaching program? (ranges)
- **Result types:** e.g., *The Optimizer, The Rebuilder, The Executive Under Pressure, The Plateaued Lifter*. Each gets a personalized breakdown of likely bottlenecks (recovery, nutrition precision, training structure, lifestyle).
- **Routing:**
  - High score (committed, ready now, budget fit) → "Apply now" or book a call.
  - Medium → walkthrough video plus a nurture email sequence.
  - Low → free resources.

### 2. "Are You Ready for Elite Coaching?" (application pre-qualifier)
**Role:** Sits directly in front of the Apply button, or replaces a long application form. It scores readiness on commitment, availability for weekly check-ins, willingness to track data, budget and timeline.
- **Benefit:** Fewer unqualified applications, and the sales team sees a score and answers before the call.
- **Tone fit:** It plays into the exclusivity ("not for everyone") the brand already projects.

### 3. "What's Holding Back Your Physique?" (diagnostic quiz)
**Role:** A lighter, more shareable quiz for ads and social traffic from his online audience.
- **Result categories:** Recovery, nutrition, training, or lifestyle bottleneck. This maps to the "One Integrated System" graphic.
- **Output:** A short personalized result plus a recommended resource, then a soft CTA to the walkthrough video.

### 4. "Longevity & Energy Score" (for the 40+ segment)
**Role:** Targets men concerned with energy, recovery and aging well ("elite output well into your 50s").
- **Use:** A way to introduce the bloodwork and data-driven side of the program.
- **Caution:** See the compliance notes below. Frame results as lifestyle insights, not hormone or medical conclusions.

## Segmentation to capture
Store these as tags or fields so they carry into the CRM or email platform:
- Goal type
- Experience level
- Budget range
- Urgency
- Interest in bloodwork or optimization (a proxy for TruGenetics add-on interest)
- Age range

## Compliance notes
- Avoid quiz results that imply a diagnosis (e.g., "you likely have low testosterone"). TRT and peptides are regulated medical topics, and Meta and Google ads are strict about health-related claims and personal-attribute targeting.
- Keep testimonial and results disclaimers consistent with the site ("individual results vary").
- Suggest results copy that points to "talk to a qualified provider" for any medical add-on.
- Add a consent checkbox for email and SMS on the lead capture step.

## Things to ask in the demo
1. Where do applicants come from today (Instagram, YouTube, ads, email)?
2. What's the current application-to-call and call-to-close rate?
3. What makes an applicant a bad fit (budget, location, goals, commitment)?
4. Is the sales team a person or a team, and where do leads currently go (CRM, calendar)?
5. Would they rather the quiz *replace* the application form or *feed into* it?
6. What are the price tiers and how do they differ? (This helps with budget-based routing.)
7. How much of the TruGenetics add-on is a revenue lever for them?
8. The site's stat blocks show "10,000 Clients coached" but the "Years of experience" and "Audience online" numbers are blank in the text. Confirm what's displayed there so quiz copy uses the right figures.

## Interact features to show
- Scored or outcome-based quiz logic with result-based routing
- Lead capture placement (before vs. after results)
- Conditional CTAs by score (Apply vs. nurture)
- Integrations or webhooks to CRM, email and calendar, with answers passed to the sales team
- Embed options, such as a quiz on the homepage in place of the "Apply" button
- Analytics on drop-off and answer distribution to refine qualification criteria
